
# M1LAB2- The Robot Sandwich

## Assumptions
- The robot is standing infront of a kitchen counter.
- The Sandwich is a PB&J.
- There is a knife, beanut butteer, jelly, bread and a plate on the counter.
- The robot is able to identify the required ingredients and tools needed.
- The robot hands are currently empty.
- None of the ingredients are open.
- The robot can choose to wash it's hands.

## Steps for the PB&J sandwich
1. Turn on the sink.
2. Place hands under the water.
3. Remove hands from water and apply soap.
4. Rub hands together with soap for 10 seconds.
5. Rinse hands off.
6. Turn off the sink.
7. Allow hands to dry.
8. Go to the counter where the ingredients and tools are.
9. Make sure there is peanut butter, jelly, knife, plate and bread.
10. Open the bag of bread.
11. Put hands into the bad and reach for 2 slices of bread.
12. Place both slices of bread on the plate.
13. Close the bag of bread and put it back on the counter.
14. Pick up the peanut butter and jelly and open both of them.
15. Twist both lids one at a time to open the lids.
16. Pick up the knife.
17. Insert the knife into the peanut butter and scoop out a fair amount of peanut butter.
18. Spread the peanut butter on one slice of bread.
19. Put the knife on the plate.
20. Grab the peanut butter and close the lid and set it aside on the counter.
21. Pick up the knife from the plate.
22. Go for the jar of jelly and scoop out a fair amount of jelly.
23. Spread the jelly onto the other slice of bread.
24. Put the knife back on the plate.
25. Close the jelly jar.
26. PIck the knife up from the plate and place it in the sink.
27. Return to the counter where the plate is at.
28. Pick up which ever slice of bread and allign it on top of the other one with the spreaded jelly or peanut butter onto the other slice of bread.
29. Release the bread when it is alligned. 
30. Place the sandwich back on the plate when complete.

## Done when
- The Peanut butter and jelly jars are closed.
- The knife is in the sink
- The plate with the sandwhich on the plate is on the counter.
- Both slices of bread have either jelly or peanut butter on one each.
- Both slices of bread are alligned together.
